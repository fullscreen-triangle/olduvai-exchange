# Synthesis after 28 papers

Read in full: 3 foundational + 8 economics + 8 epistemology + 7 Buhera + 2 earlier.
~36,000 lines. This note is what survives cross-checking — the convergences, the
contradictions, and the shape Olduvai appears to want.

**Still outstanding:** `sachikonye2025knowledge` (the bridge-theorem paper), and the
entire physics/technical half.

## 1. The spine, restated once

Every paper that carries the floor derives or reuses the same chain:

**non-completability + proper parthood ⟹ β > 0 ⟹ individuation by negation ⟹
region-not-point ⟹ a gate is required to select ⟹ no return ⟹ one gate per coherent
collective ⟹ authored structures are bounded.**

The floor has now been derived **six independent ways** across the corpus: from
non-completability of a whole; from finite memory `|K| < |X|`; from
non-completability of a search medium; from cardinality of a knowledge framework;
from an unfinishable market comparison; from the Fisher metric diverging at the
boundary. **Six routes to one constant is the strongest structural evidence in the
corpus.**

## 2. What the corpus AGREES on — six convergences

These recur across papers written in different vocabularies, often without
cross-citation. Convergence under independent derivation is the real signal.

### 2.1 Price is a band. This is not a metaphor and not a modelling choice.
`foundation-for-price`: price is a cell; the sticker is a carrier; **WTP is the slack
`τ(C) − β`.** `economic-agent-theory`: cell diameter **IS** the bid-ask spread; β is
the **minimum achievable spread**. `shadrach`: the frictionless zero-spread market is
**not expressible** — a compile-time type error. `market-equilibrium`: fundamental
value is a cell of width `2·S♭,∞` **even with infinitely many agents.**

**⟹ Olduvai quotes `[p−τ, p+τ]`, publishes τ, and treats a point price as a category
error.** The tick size is not a convention — it is `β`, and it is measurable.

### 2.2 Heterogeneity is productive, and the mechanism is multiplicative.
`heterogeneity-theorem`: the composite floor is a **product**, products are
**Schur-concave**, so spreading floors at fixed mean strictly shrinks the floor.
Homogeneous = **unique worst case**. `market-equilibrium`: two identical agents
compose no better than one; **independence, not repetition, buys the reduction.**
`knowledge-thermodynamics`: **weak replication strictly dominates strong.**
`coordinate-theory-of-advertising`: heterogeneous decoders make disagreement
*forced*, not accidental.

**⟹ Recruit for decoder-disjointness, not headcount. Membership screens are
information taxes. Never re-inspect the same way twice.**

### 2.3 Coordination needs a shared outcome space, not shared beliefs.
`agent-coordination` proves it (Common-Cell Convergence places **no condition on the
`Kᵢ`**); `market-equilibrium` proves the market version (**"this incompatibility is
*why* trade occurs"**); `epistemological-mode-equivalence` extends it to evidence
routes; `split-attention` gives the timing cost.

**⟹ The contract spec is the entire coordination requirement.** Build UIs in each
participant's native representation; **do not force a common vocabulary.** No
consensus protocol, no trusted oracle, no common-knowledge hierarchy.

### 2.4 Closure/decline is a first-class correct outcome.
`semantic-causal-propagation`: **convergent closure or honest decline**; closure is
*strictly stronger* than any confidence threshold. `market-equilibrium`: the
no-purpose region `τ ≤ S♭` where **no equilibrium exists**. `agent-instantiation`:
**STRUCTURE-LIMITED as an emitted typed signal** — "a stalled agent does not fall
silent."

**⟹ A third settlement state: "contested — no equilibrium." Publish the
disagreement, never a synthesised price.** And make illiquidity an emitted signal,
because *absence of trades and structural failure look identical otherwise.*

### 2.5 Histories do not return, and this is the settlement ledger.
Present in every paper that carries the floor. `shadrach`: **"an 'undo' is itself a
further cut."** `split-attention`: **"a cached reply from session 1 returned in
session 47 is a different, earlier character speaking out of turn."** `blank-screen`:
trajectory monotonicity as a **runtime-enforced global invariant** with no reverse
operation in the dispatch table.

**⟹ Append-only ledger with compensating entries. A bust is a new act at a higher
count. Never `UPDATE trades SET status=...`.** And **never cache a quote, price,
margin, or fill decision** — in a produce market the state changes constantly, so a
cached basis quote *is formally the answer to a different market.*

### 2.6 Endpoint audit cannot catch a bridge. Route audit is mandatory.
`coordinate-theory-of-advertising`: **no function of the endpoint holonomies
determines the bridge holonomy** — route-audit is necessary *and* sufficient.
`semantic-causal-propagation`: path opacity says the interior is invisible to
endpoint tests — **which is *why* bridges survive.** `shadrach`: `Admit` inspects
**only terminal convergence** — a pure endpoint audit *by explicit design*.
`kirchoff-residual`: the store-then-ship vs ship-then-store loop is the canonical
agricultural arbitrage, and **no single-contract audit finds it.**

**⟹ Two audit numbers per session: node balance `max|rᵢ|` AND cycle holonomy
`max_C |∮f|` normalised by carry. Alert when `|∮f| > Σ ω_e` — friction is β, and
β becomes the no-alert band.**

## 3. What the corpus DISAGREES on — four real tensions

Recording these because they are decision points, not noise.

### 3.1 ⚠️ Is separation free or costly? (Buhera vs everything else)
`os-system` / `architecture`: `W_cat = 0` — categorical operations cost nothing.
Everything else: `β > 0` is the whole foundation.

**Resolution for Olduvai: the floored reading wins, and the reconciliation is
`vaHera`'s aperture.** Re-sorting/re-grading/re-bundling *is* free — **and creates
nothing.** Value is created only at the open aperture where the physical work
happens. **⟹ Charge fees at the aperture, not at the sort.** This is not a
compromise; it is the sharper statement.

### 3.2 ⚠️ Point-convergence vs region-conservation
Buhera's partition refinement converges **to a point**; the penultimate state is
**unique**. T2/T3 forbid exactly this.

**Resolution: keep the region.** Buhera's own floored papers agree — `S♭ > 0`,
`im(S) ⊆ [S♭, 100]`, the metric singularity means `S_k = 0` is "approached
asymptotically but never exactly attained." **Termination is always at a block of
extent `1/k`, never a point.**

### 3.3 ⚠️ Path opacity vs route audit
Not actually contradictory, but the resolution matters: **path opacity is why the
route must be *recorded*, not why it can be ignored.** The trajectory is invisible to
*endpoint tests*; it is perfectly visible if you *log it*.
**⟹ An accountable exchange must record routes, not just outcomes.** Architectural
requirement, not a feature.

### 3.4 ⚠️ Water-filling vs concentration
`split-attention`: **water-filling is optimal only in a concave-return environment.
Where returns increase, the optimal agent CONCENTRATES.**

**This is the sharpest practical warning in the corpus.** Agricultural markets
routinely present increasing returns: a thin market that only becomes tradeable once
enough presence *makes* it liquid; a delivery network where partial infrastructure is
worth far less than complete; a benchmark price where half a benchmark is not half as
useful.

**⟹ Classify contracts by gain-profile curvature and run TWO policies.** Mature
contracts in the water-filling pool; **bootstrap contracts pulled OUT and given
dedicated undivided budget — because if left in, the single-price rule will correctly
compute they deserve ~0 and they will never launch.** The scheduler isn't
miscomputing; *it is optimising an objective that does not describe the situation.*

## 4. What the corpus does NOT provide

Recording honestly, because these are the gaps Olduvai must fill or route around.

1. **No mechanism design anywhere.** No incentive compatibility, no IR, no budget
   balance, no revelation principle, **no strategic agents at all** — agents decode
   and project, they never misreport. Arrow appears in a bibliography and is never
   engaged. **The corpus gives contract geometry and nothing about incentives.**
2. **No fraud, collusion, or adversarial model.** β is *noise*, not *lying*. A
   corrupt grader is not a high-β receiver; he is outside the model. **And collusion
   destroys exactly the independence every composition theorem requires.**
3. **No empirical validation of anything.** Every "45/45 pass at 1e-15" verifies that
   closed forms evaluate to themselves in IEEE-754. **No real market data anywhere in
   28 papers.** The most commercially decisive claim (flat scaling) rests on boot
   sizes 5–40.
4. **Time is largely absent.** `shadrach` has no `t` at all — futures, expiry, carry,
   seasonality inexpressible. `kirchoff-residual` has horizons but assumes
   stationarity, which agriculture violently violates.
5. **Provenance is structurally invisible to the trading receiver.** Path opacity
   means traceability is **not recoverable from the exchange's own evaluation map.**
   For export certification, organic claims, or deforestation compliance you must
   **build provenance as a separate receiver with its own floor.**
6. **Multi-stakeholder negotiation and arbitration are excluded by the papers' own
   scope conditions.** Route to humans.
7. **`f*` — the truth value every S-transform requires — is never constructed.**

## 5. The design that falls out

Not a proposal — an observation about what the constraints jointly force.

### The measurement that everything hangs on
**Measure β empirically: re-inspect the same lot with independent
inspectors/instruments; the spread of resulting classifications IS β.** Tick size,
grade bands, dispute thresholds, arbitration tolerance, listing validation, matching
gate — all calibrate off this one number, and **the corpus gives no value for it.**

### Forced by the theorems
| Constraint | Source | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Quote in bands, publish τ | universal | no point prices anywhere in the schema |
| Tick = β | price/agent papers | endogenous, not decreed |
| Grade bands > field-instrument floor | price/mode-equivalence | **delist grades that fail** |
| New grade must be **cell-disjoint** | knowledge-thermo / operational-intelligence | automated listing gate, decidable |
| `τ(C) > S♭(E)` | market-equilibrium | **listing validator**; below it *no equilibrium exists* |
| Matching gate `β_B + β_S ≤ 2τ` | foundation-for-price | refuse matches that will dispute at delivery |
| Batched call auctions | knowledge-thermo (phase lock) + synchronised | discovery and execution never concurrent |
| Monotone order updates | market-equilibrium | monotonicity is the *hypothesis* of convergence |
| Append-only, no rollback | T6, universal | busts are new acts |
| Route recording | advertising + kirchoff | the only way to catch bridges |
| ≥3 independent validators | unconstrained-subtasks | **two-party attestation provably insufficient** |
| One interface at every tier | unconstrained-subtasks | village = national, or lose the advantage |
| Single shared grading standard `K` | agent-theory | **two standards = two markets wearing one name** |

### The two metrics no exchange publishes
**`Δᵀ = 2·min βᵢ`** — cost to trade, set by the single best participant.
**`Δᴵ = 2·∏βᵢ/Σⁿ⁻¹`** — how well the market *knows*, set by all participants.

**Different budgets, different returns.** Precision investment (reference lab, bonded
warehouse, certified assay) buys `Δᵀ` and discovery speed. Breadth and heterogeneity
buy `Δᴵ` and reference-price accuracy. **Most agricultural exchange projects fund
only the first and then wonder why their reference price isn't trusted.**

Worked magnitude: floors {10, 20, 35} → aggregate 10, composite ≈0.7. **A 14× gap
between what the best trader knows and what the market collectively knows.**

### What Olduvai could report that nothing else does
- **The price cell width** — an honesty statement about what the market knows.
- **`Δᴵ` alongside `Δᵀ`** — collective vs individual knowledge.
- **Cycle holonomy** — the bridge detector.
- **`Θ = ∫|G|`** — price *discovery* rather than throughput. **Volume measures
  logistics; Θ measures what a market uniquely provides.** Decisive for thin markets
  where volume-based metrics make an efficient exchange look moribund.
- **`R_ens`** — a herding alarm. Rising toward lock means price discovery has stopped
  and stampeding has started.
- **Effective independent participant count**, not headcount.
- **Nightly single-dropout resilience** per contract.
- **Observed / Retrieved / Synthesized** on every published price.

## 6. The research question — candidates, sharpened

[[00-framing]] asked what the apparatus is *for*. Four candidates now have teeth:

**(a) Is price a cell rather than a point, and does that predict observables a
point-price model does not?** The corpus hands you the two-pronged test:
**resolution never saturates** and **independent instruments never become
redundant.** Both measurable in a market. This is the most falsifiable claim
available, and it is the author's own framing rather than my inference.

**(b) ⭐ Is an exchange a bridge-detector?** True farmgate price + true retail price +
an unaudited intermediate route labelled "the farmer benefits." Every figure checks
out; only route-audit finds it. **The corpus proves endpoint audit *cannot* work.**
If Olduvai's function is making routes visible and auditable, that is a research
contribution and a public good, not a product feature.

**(c) Does contested closure produce more useful output than a printed price?**
Publishing "the sources disagree, here are the distinct classes" at exactly the
moments a false price does most damage — thin contracts, post-shock sessions, a lost
critical participant. **A product would never ship a market that can refuse to
print. The research framing licenses it.**

**(d) Does heterogeneity-driven inclusion measurably improve reference-price
accuracy?** `Π ≈ Σ(1−e^(−n·CV²/2))(β̄/Σ)`. **Smallholder inclusion stops being a
welfare cost traded against efficiency and becomes the mechanism by which the
reference price becomes accurate.** Directly testable by varying roster CV.

**These are not mutually exclusive.** (b) and (c) are the same instrument seen from
two sides — both are about refusing to fabricate resolution the structure does not
support.
